# Social Poster

Prepare short-form social-media post packages from existing content assets.

## Intended Use

This skill accepts prepared source content such as an article summary, campaign brief, announcement, or launch note and turns it into channel-ready social copy.

## Inputs

- `request`: source content, brief, or posting instructions.
- Optional platform context such as target audience, tone, or channel priorities.

## Outputs

- Platform-ready social post copy.
- Suggested caption variants and hooks.
- Short publishing notes or call-to-action phrasing when requested.

## Workflow

1. Read the source request and identify the core message.
2. Compress the message into a concise social format.
3. Adjust tone and structure for lightweight distribution scenarios.
4. Return one or more posting variants suitable for publication review.

## Usage Notes

- Best used after a longer-form draft, article, or campaign message already exists.
- Keep the request focused on one campaign or content item at a time.
- Provide platform or audience hints when channel-specific phrasing matters.
