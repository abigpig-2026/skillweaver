# Blog Writer Tips

This note accompanies the `blog-writer` supporting skill bundled in the
SkillWeaver submission artifact.

## Editorial Reminders

- Keep each article focused on one topic and one audience.
- Prefer concrete examples over generic filler.
- Use headings to separate problem framing, analysis, and action items.
- Place the main keyword naturally in the title, introduction, and at least one subheading.
- End with a short next step or call to action when the workflow expects conversion-oriented copy.

## Review Checklist

- Is the title specific and readable?
- Does the introduction explain why the topic matters?
- Are the body sections logically ordered?
- Does the article avoid unsupported claims?
- Is the conclusion consistent with the target audience and style?
